Meter setting

Select GR on the Peak Limiter 76 meter to monitor gain reduction. Press the 4 ratio button (4:1). Start with Attack 3 and Release 5.

How to dial it in

  1. Ask the drummer to play Kick In at normal worship performance intensity.
  2. Set the SQ-5 preamp gain for healthy headroom without clipping.
  3. Increase the Peak Limiter 76 Input until strong kick hits show approximately 3–5 dB gain reduction on the GR meter.
  4. Adjust Output until the compressed and bypassed levels sound similar.
  5. Listen with Kick Out and bass DI in the mix; confirm kick microphone polarity and that the low end remains punchy.

Fine-tuning: On this model, higher Attack and Release numbers mean faster action. If the beater loses its attack, try a slower attack. If compression pumps between hits, adjust release or reduce input drive. These are starting points, not a saved console preset.

Kick OutOpto
CompressorOpto

Browse add-on descriptions ↓

Meter setting

Watch the model’s gain-reduction indication (not just its output meter); use the gentle optical starting settings already listed.

How to dial it in

  1. Play Kick Out at a realistic performance level, including its loudest expected moments.
  2. Check the input/preamp or bus feed for healthy headroom and no clipping.
  3. Lower Threshold or increase compression using the model’s controls until the target GR appears. Aim for about 2–3 dB of gain reduction on louder moments; do not force constant reduction.
  4. Use the model’s Gain control to match bypassed loudness.
  5. Listen in the full mix for low-frequency weight and Kick In combined; check Kick In/Kick Out polarity and avoid low-end pumping.

Fine-tuning: Optical recovery is program-dependent; listen for pumping and excessive sustain. These are soundcheck starting points, not fixed presets.

Meter setting

Leave the channel or bus compressor OUT / bypassed. Check the SQ input/output level meters for clipping and confirm that no gain reduction is being applied here.

How to verify it

  1. Play the hi-hat in the full drum mix and confirm the compressor remains bypassed.
  2. Set preamp gain with adequate headroom; check cymbal and snare bleed.
  3. Listen for a natural hi-hat decay without pumping.
  4. If level changes are needed, use fader/EQ first rather than automatically adding compression.

Fine-tuning: Bypass is the recommended starting state for this path. Only enable compression later to solve a clearly identified mixing problem.
